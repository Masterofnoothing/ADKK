# Project Overview

This freelance concept project produced a high-fidelity **Self-Balancing 2-Arm Robot** suitable for marketing, pitch presentations, and mechanical assembly reference.

![Self-balancing robot model](images/self-balancing-robot-3d.jpg)

---

# Design & Modeling Process

### Blockout and Scale

Primitive cylinders and boxes defined the proportions of the wheels, gyroscope housing, and twin robotic arms.

### Hard-Surface Refinement

Bevel modifiers, Boolean operations, panel lines, wiring guides, and PBR materials were used to convey a functional industrial design.

The final renders used Cycles with studio lighting and subtle depth of field.
